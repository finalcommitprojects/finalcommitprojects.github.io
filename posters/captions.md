## 10 · A4 flyer (print)
File: `10-flyer-A4-print.pdf`. Print it on A4. Colour looks best, but it still works in black and white.

- The QR code opens a chat that says "I saw your poster on the notice board".
- Cut along the dotted lines at the bottom so students can tear off the number.
- Good places, with permission: department notice boards, hostel boards, the library, and the xerox/print shops near colleges, where students print their reports.

## Posting plan (first two weeks)

| Day | Poster | Where |
|---|---|---|
| 1 | 01 deadline | Your Status + 2 or 3 class groups |
| 3 | 02 register | Status + different groups |
| 5 | 03 viva | Status + groups |
| 7 | 04 checklist | Status |
| 9 | 05 idea | Status + groups |
| 11 | 06 sticky | Status |
| 13 | 01 deadline again | Status + new groups |

- In Karnataka college groups, post the Kannada version on the same day instead of the English one. Don't post both in the same group.
- Post Status between 8 and 10 pm, when students are online.
- Ask the admin before posting in a college group, and post no more than one poster per group per week. Spam gets you removed.
- Ask friends and juniors at other colleges to put the poster on their Status. That reaches people you can't.
- After two weeks, count the first messages by poster ("I saw your viva poster…") and post more of whichever worked.
